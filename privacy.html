What we collect

  • Account: email address and password (handled by our authentication provider), display name, handle, bio, helmet color choice, genres, optional group code.
  • Location: precise location while the app is open and you have granted permission, used for the map, ride tracking and (if you enable it) sharing your position with other riders. You choose visibility: Off, Friends, Group, or Everyone (approximate, rounded to a radius you set).
  • Ride data: time, distance, speed, elevation, route and optional heart rate. Stored on your device and, when signed in, in your account.
  • Photos and videos: saved on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers in this version.
  • Marketplace and cause posts: items and events you create, visible publicly or to your group as you choose.

How we use it

To provide the app features above. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Third parties

  • Supabase (authentication, database, realtime).
  • Map tiles from OpenStreetMap contributors and CARTO.
  • OpenStreetMap-based routing service when you request directions (your start and destination coordinates are sent).

Camera, microphone and Bluetooth

Used only when you record video or connect a heart-rate monitor. Recordings stay on your device.

Your choices

You can turn location sharing off in Profile → Settings & privacy, delete listings/causes you created, sign out, or request deletion of your account and data by emailing the contact above.

Children

Pedalz is not directed to children under 13.
